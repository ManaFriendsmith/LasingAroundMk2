# Basic Features #

- Helium is a byproduct of oil processing, available at blue science.
- Helium is used to craft lasers, used in a variety of mid- and lategame technology.
- Adds the laser mill, an assembling machine variant that can craft a variety of recipes with bonus productivity but consumes helium to do so.

# Series #

This mod is part of the More Materials series. Similar to the BZ series, each mod adds a resource to the game and you can mix and match them to achieve your desired level of complexity.

- [Brass Tacks](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/BrassTacksMk2) - Zinc, brass, mechanical parts
- [If I Had a Nickel](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/IfNickelMk2) - Nickel, invar, motors and valves
- [Them Thar Hills](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/ThemTharHillsMk2) - Gold and electronics
- [BrimStuff](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/BrimStuffMk2) - Sulfur and chemistry
- [LasingAround](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/LasingAroundMk2) - Helium and lasers

# Difficulty Breakdown #

All More Materials mods provide different difficulty levels, controlled by a mod setting in the PF functions dependency.

## Minimal Difficulty ##

Adds helium and lasers. Aside from the handful of recipes that consume helium directly, helium is used as a sort of wildcard resource to add productivity wherever you need it with the laser mill.

In non-space-age, low density structures can only be crafted in the laser mill.

Spectroscopes are crafted from lasers and used for space research or to boost the output of oil refining.

Lasers are needed to craft rocket parts and, in Space Age, can be found from recycling scrap. Since helium is only directly available on Nauvis and Aquilo, it's impossible to make Gleba and Vulcanus fully self-sufficient. (There are promethium-tech recipes that allow crafting lasers without helium if you're into that sort of thing.)

## Moderate Difficulty ##

Adds scanners, an advanced electronic intermediate.

With Brass Tacks and Them Thar Hills, adds trackers.

With If I Had A Nickel and non-space-age, adds micron-tolerance components, which must be crafted in the laser mill.

In Space Age, adds Weird Alien Gizmos, a secondary type of scrap obtained from scrap that recycles into a generally more useful pool of items. They benefit from scrap recycling productivity, so the ratio of chaff to valuable products on Fulgora gets slightly better over time!

## Maximal Difficulty ##

No changes to non-space-age.

Adds the Cardinal Grammeter, an advanced intermediate product crafted on Fulgora.

Adds the Antiparticle Decelerator, a mysterious piece of precursor technology. By harnessing the unstable annihilation reaction of helium and batteries it transforms an unlikely array of items into the components of the Newtronic [sic] Chip, the basis of all quantum computation.
